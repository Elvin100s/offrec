How it works

Three steps. Then you're recording.

  1. 1

    Pick what to record

    A single window, a browser tab or your entire screen. Your browser asks which one, and only that gets recorded.

  2. 2

    Place your camera

    Snap it to any corner, drag it anywhere and resize it from its corners. Turn it off any time without losing your place.

  3. 3

    Record and save

    A 3-second countdown gives you time to switch windows. Pause whenever you like, then name the video and keep it.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Camera in any corner

Your camera sits on top of your screen, mirrored like a selfie view. Four corner presets, free dragging and resizing.

Floating controls

A small Pause and Stop window stays on top of the app you're recording, and never shows up in the video. In Chrome and Edge.

Review, name, save

Watch your recording straight away, give it a name and save it to your computer. Save as many copies as you like.

Pause without gaps

Pause and resume as often as you need. The pauses are cut out, so the finished video plays straight through.

Your voice and the tab's sound

Record your microphone together with audio from a shared tab. Mute and unmute mid-recording with one key.

Keeps going in the background

Switch to the window you're presenting. Offrec keeps recording smoothly even when its own tab is out of sight.

Privacy

Off the record, by design.

Offrec has no server to send your video to. Capturing, mixing and saving all happen inside your browser, on your computer.

  • No uploads. Your recordings only exist where you save them.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
  • No tracking. No cookies, no analytics, no fonts or scripts from other companies.
  • Open source. Every line is on GitHub for anyone to check.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Offrec is open source under the MIT license. There's no account, no watermark, no time limit and no paid tier.

Where do my recordings go?

Only to your computer. When you're done you watch the recording, give it a name and save it as a video file. Nothing is ever uploaded.

How long can I record?

There's no time limit. The recording is kept in your browser until you save it, so up to about an hour is comfortable on most computers. At most it takes about 2.3 GB per hour, and still screens like slides are much smaller.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer. The floating controls need Chrome or Edge. Phones and tablets can't capture their screen from a web page.

Can it record sound?

Your microphone, plus the audio of a browser tab when you tick "Share audio" in the browser's picker. Some browsers also offer system audio when you share your entire screen.

What format are the videos?

MP4 where your browser can record it, otherwise WebM. Both play in browsers and in players like VLC.

Why aren't there floating controls when I record my entire screen?

Because they'd end up in your video. A window or tab recording only captures that window or tab, so the controls can float on top safely. For a full-screen recording, come back to the Offrec tab to pause or stop.
